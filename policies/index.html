Privacy Policy

historyLast updated: March 25, 2026

Munetios is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or monetize personal data. Any information you provide is strictly optional and only supplied when you actively choose to use a feature that requires it.

We do not perform behavioral tracking, profiling, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking. Your activity is not monitored or analyzed.

While Munetios itself does not collect personal data, some features may require requests to third-party services. These services operate under their own privacy policies and may process limited technical data as part of standard web communication.

Third-Party Requests & External Services

Munetios minimizes external requests wherever possible. However, certain features such as search results, content previews, and media rendering may require communication with third-party services.

These requests are performed only when required to deliver functionality requested by the user and are never used for advertising, profiling, or tracking by Munetios.

When a third-party request occurs, the external service may receive:

  • IP address
  • Browser user agent
  • Request headers

This information is part of standard internet communication. Some third-party services may independently collect telemetry or logs in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Munetios does not send personal identifiers, account data, or behavioral tracking information to third parties.

Where possible, Munetios applies privacy-enhancing techniques such as:

  • Proxying requests through Munetios infrastructure
  • Reducing or removing referrer data
  • Limiting metadata exposure

Search Data Sources

Munetios Search aggregates publicly available data from multiple sources to provide useful and relevant results.

Sources may include:

  • GitHub
  • Wikipedia
  • Hacker News
  • Minecraft Wiki
  • Stack Overflow
  • Reddit
  • DuckDuckGo Instant Answers API
  • Wikidata
  • dev.to

Requests to these services may be sent directly or through Munetios infrastructure depending on the feature.

Each provider operates independently and may collect limited technical data such as request logs or telemetry.

Munetios does not enable cross-site tracking and does not combine data from these services into user profiles.

Third-Party Images & Media

Munetios may display images, thumbnails, and icons from third-party providers to improve usability and presentation.

Examples include:

  • Google Favicons service (site icons)
  • Wikimedia / Wikipedia images
  • YouTube thumbnail images
  • OpenFreeMap Tiles

When these resources are loaded, the provider may receive standard request data such as IP address and request headers.

These resources are used only for visual display. Munetios does not embed tracking pixels or hidden identifiers within media.

Any data collection performed by third-party media providers is governed by their own privacy policies.

Content Delivery

Munetios may load static resources such as stylesheets, icons, or scripts from trusted content delivery networks (CDNs) to ensure performance and compatibility.

Examples may include:

  • Google Fonts
  • jsDelivr
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com
  • unpkg

These services may receive limited technical information required to deliver content but are not used by Munetios for tracking or analytics.

AI Features

Munetios includes optional AI-powered features designed to improve productivity and usability.

AI interactions are not used for training by default and are not retained beyond the active session unless explicitly stated.

Munetios does not use AI to profile users or infer personal traits.

Any optional data sharing for improvement purposes will be clearly disclosed and fully controlled by the user.

Speech Recognition

Munetios uses the Web Speech API for voice input features.

Speech processing is handled by your browser or operating system. Munetios does not process or store voice recordings.

Voice input is converted to text and used only for the requested action.

Encryption

Munetios uses end-to-end encryption where applicable. Data is encrypted on your device and cannot be accessed by Munetios servers.

Encryption keys are managed locally and are never shared.

Cookies

Munetios uses minimal cookies strictly for essential functionality such as preferences and session handling.

Cookies are not used for tracking, analytics, or advertising.

COPPA

Munetios does not collect personal data and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

GDPR

Munetios follows GDPR principles by design. Because no personal data is collected or stored, user rights are inherently respected.

GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), Munetios complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Local-Only Products: For products that store data only on your device, no personal data is processed on Munetios servers. As a result, GDPR rights such as data deletion, correction, or portability are satisfied by default and are not applicable at the service level.

Planned Account-Based Services: Munetios Accounts and other account-based services are planned but not yet active. When these services launch and involve server-side processing of personal data, Munetios will provide appropriate tools to exercise GDPR rights, including access, correction, deletion, and data export.

Munetios relies on GDPR Article 6(1)(b) (service necessity) and Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest for security and functionality) where applicable. Munetios does not use consent-based tracking technologies.

If you are located in Europe, please use Munetios New Search instead of Classic Search to ensure full compliance with regional regulations.
