Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 22, 2026

Data Collection

Munetios is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or monetize personal data. Any information you provide is strictly optional and only supplied when you actively choose to use a specific feature that requires it. We do not engage in background data collection, behavioral profiling, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking.

Munetios does not use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or external telemetry systems. We do not track how you navigate the site, what pages you visit, or how long you stay. All usage remains private by default.

When data is voluntarily provided, it is used solely for its intended purpose, processed securely, and never shared with third parties. We routinely review our data practices to ensure they align with modern privacy expectations and applicable data protection laws.

AI Features

Munetios includes optional AI-powered features designed to assist with productivity, accessibility, and usability. By default, AI interactions are not used to train models and are not stored beyond the active session. We do not use AI systems to profile users, infer personal traits, or build behavioral datasets.

If an AI feature offers optional data sharing for improvement or training purposes, this will be clearly communicated to you in advance. You retain full control over whether such sharing is enabled and may change your preferences at any time. Opting out does not limit access to core functionality.

All AI processing is handled securely, and personal data is never retained without explicit user consent. Transparency is central to how our AI systems operate, and we aim to make their behavior understandable and predictable.

Speech Recognition

Munetios uses the Web Speech Recognition API to enable voice-based input for features such as search and maps navigation. This functionality allows spoken words to be converted into text to improve accessibility and ease of use.

Speech recognition is handled by your browser or operating system using its built-in speech services. Munetios does not operate its own speech recognition servers and does not directly process or store voice audio. Any voice data handling is governed by the privacy policies of your browser or device provider.

Munetios does not record, retain, log, or transmit voice recordings to its servers. Once speech input is converted into text, it is used only for the immediate action requested by the user, such as performing a search or locating a place on the map.

Use of speech recognition is entirely optional. You may disable microphone access at any time through your browser or device settings. Munetios recommends reviewing your browser’s speech and microphone permissions to ensure they align with your privacy preferences.

Encryption

Munetios uses end-to-end encryption by default and at all times. All user data is encrypted on your device before it is transmitted and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient or by you on your own device. Munetios does not have access to unencrypted user content.

Encryption keys are generated and managed locally and are never shared with Munetios servers. This design ensures that even in the event of unauthorized access to infrastructure, user data remains unreadable and protected.

In addition to end-to-end encryption, all communications between your device and Munetios services are protected using modern, industry-standard transport encryption protocols. This provides layered security against interception, tampering, and unauthorized access.

Because Munetios cannot decrypt end-to-end encrypted content, we are unable to view, analyze, or recover private user data. Responsibility for data access and security remains entirely with the user, reinforcing Munetios’s commitment to privacy-by-design.

Cookies

Munetios uses a minimal number of cookies strictly for essential functionality. These cookies are limited to session management, preference storage, and basic feature operation. We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or tracking purposes.

Cookie data is never shared with third parties and is not used to identify or profile users. Examples of cookie usage include remembering login sessions, theme preferences, or language settings.

You have full control over cookies through your browser settings and may disable them at any time. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect certain features, but Munetios will continue to respect your privacy choices.

COPPA

Munetios complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Our services are not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Munetios does not collect, store, or process personal data and does not require user accounts. As a result, Munetios does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Because no personal data is collected, there is no personal information to retain, share, or sell. Munetios does not engage in behavioral tracking, profiling, or analytics that could involve children’s data.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that information related to a child has been inadvertently provided to Munetios, they may contact us to request review. Any such information, if found, will be promptly deleted.

GDPR

Munetios complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and is committed to protecting the privacy rights of users in the European Union (EU) and European Economic Area (EEA). Privacy-by-design is a core principle of Munetios.

Munetios does not collect, store, or process personal data and does not require user accounts. As a result, no personal data is retained, profiled, or shared, and GDPR data protection principles are inherently upheld by the service architecture.

Because Munetios does not collect personal data, user rights such as access, rectification, deletion, restriction, and objection are satisfied by default. There is no stored personal information for Munetios to access, modify, or erase.

If you have questions regarding GDPR compliance or believe an issue related to personal data has occurred, you may contact us through our designated data protection channels for review.
