Meet Munetios Search our private search engine

Munetios Search is a private search engine that does not track you. We respect your privacy and do not collect any personal data from our users.

Lets compare other search engines

  • Munetios Search – No ads, respects privacy, does not track you, open source, no accounts needed
  • Google Search – Has ads, tracks you, not open source, accounts optional (but needed for personalization)
  • Bing Search – Has ads, tracks you, not open source, accounts optional (but needed for personalization)
  • DuckDuckGo – No ads (in most regions), does not track you, not open source, no accounts needed
  • Brave Search – Has ads (optional), does not track you, partially open source, no accounts needed
