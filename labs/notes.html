About This App

This is an experimental productivity application with end-to-end encryption. All your notes are encrypted using industry-standard AES-GCM encryption before being stored locally in IndexedDB.

How It Works

  • Notes are encrypted with a unique key stored securely in your browser
  • Encryption happens in your browser - data never leaves your device
  • All data is stored locally in IndexedDB
  • No server communication - complete privacy

Features

  • End-to-end encrypted notes
  • Local storage with IndexedDB
  • Beautiful liquid glass design
  • Fully functional - no placeholder elements
